📘 Understand it & how to advocate
📗 In plain terms

This is the page that promises exactly what services Ethan gets — how many minutes, how often, who delivers them, and where. It's the single most important page for holding the school accountable: if a service isn't delivered, you point to these numbers to prove it.

⚠️ Why this falls short
  1. 1OT dropped from 60 → 10 min/month and from direct to consult-only, with no data-based reason — exactly the unexplained cut Endrew F. and the CDE guidance flag.
  2. 2"At least 10 minutes" sets a floor with no ceiling and no schedule — unenforceable, because any amount technically satisfies it.
  3. 3No dedicated 1:1 minutes are written anywhere, though his present levels document writing fatigue and fine-motor difficulty.
❓ Questions to ask — in this order
1
Start
"Can you walk me through, service by service, exactly how many minutes Ethan gets, who delivers each one — a licensed special educator, the interventionist, or a paraprofessional — and in what setting?"
2
If the answer is vague
"His OT went from 60 minutes direct to 10 minutes consult-only. What regression or progress data supported that reduction, and can you show it to me?"
3
If they won't commit
"Under 34 CFR §300.320(a)(7), services have to state minutes, frequency, provider, and setting so they're clear enough to implement. I'm asking that we write the specific numbers in today, and that any reduction and its data reasoning go in the Prior Written Notice."
🎯 Your final ask
The change to request
  1. 1Restore direct OT to 60 min/month (or state the specific amount his data supports), delivered one-on-one by a licensed OT, not consult-only.
  2. 2Replace "at least 10 minutes" with an exact figure, frequency, provider role, and setting — no open-ended floors.
  3. 3Add dedicated 1:1 instructional minutes for written work, tied to the writing-fatigue and fine-motor needs named in his present levels.
  4. 4Put any reduction, and the data behind it, into the Prior Written Notice.
Why this fits Ethan
  1. 1His present levels already document writing fatigue, slow handwriting, and fine-motor difficulty — the exact needs OT and 1:1 support address.
  2. 2A service can't be shown as delivered or missed unless its minutes, provider, and setting are specific — that specificity is your only proof later.
The law behind it

Services must state minutes, frequency, provider, and setting clearly enough to implement, and a change to FAPE needs written notice with its data. 34 CFR §300.320(a)(7) §300.503 (PWN) Endrew F.

📄 Exact language to put in the IEP

Paste these straight into an email or an amendment request — no editing needed.

  1. Occupational therapy: 60 minutes per month of direct, one-on-one service, provided by a licensed occupational therapist, in a separate setting.
  2. Specialized instruction in written expression: 30 minutes, 3× per week, in a small group (ratio no greater than 1:5), delivered by a special educator in the resource room.
  3. Dedicated 1:1 support for extended writing tasks: 20 minutes per school day, provided by a special educator or trained paraprofessional under the special educator's direction.
  4. Progress on each service will be reported to the parent every 9 weeks, with data, concurrent with report cards.
📈 How progress gets tracked + services that back it up
