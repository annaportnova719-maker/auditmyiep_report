The words are the whole game

An IEP is a legal promise — but a promise only counts if it's specific. Schools often use soft language that sounds supportive and commits to nothing. When the words are vague, it's much harder to hold the school accountable for what your child was promised.

"support as needed" "access to extra time" "will be encouraged to" "consult as appropriate" "making progress" "when the student self-advocates"

Questions parents ask

Is this legal advice?

No. AuditMyIEP is an educational tool to help you understand your child's IEP and prepare to advocate. It doesn't replace a special-education attorney or advocate. For legal advice about your specific situation, talk to a licensed attorney in your state.

Is my child's information safe?

Yes. Your IEP is never saved to any system or AI platform and never used to train AI. It's read only to build your report, then cleared the moment the report is ready, and only you ever see it. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

What if my IEP is long?

That's fine — IEPs are often 30 to 60 pages. The audit handles the whole document; longer files just take a little longer to process.

How accurate is it?

The report is generated by AI checking your IEP against federal standards. It's a strong starting point for understanding and advocating — not a guarantee of any outcome. Always review it against your own knowledge of your child.

Do you store my payment information?

No. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details — only a confirmation that the payment went through.
